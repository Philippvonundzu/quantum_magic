\begin{frame}{Conclusions}
  \small
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \column{0.5\textwidth}
    \begin{block}{Key insights}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Magic = distance from the stabilizer polytope; all good measures lie between $\Dmin$ and $\LR$.
        \item ``One unit of magic per qubit'': max and typical $\approx n$.
        \item Hypergraph states: magic = how far a Boolean function is from quadratic.
        \item Too much magic is useless: power needs structure.
        \item SPT phases: extensive but medium magic.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
    \column{0.47\textwidth}
    \begin{alertblock}{Challenges \& outlook}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Computing magic: $2^{\Theta(n^2)}$ vertices --- need easier bounds.
        \item Explicit nearly-maximal magic states?
        \item Do random circuits ($O(n)$-designs) reach maximal magic?
        \item Magic vs.\ entanglement: does one force the other?
        \item Magic as a diagnostic of phases: MBQC universality, sign problem, topological order.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{alertblock}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}
